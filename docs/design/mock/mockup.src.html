1 · Two graph styles

Simple looks like the Karoo's own elevation graph (a line with a see-through fill), but in the Karoo's climb blue instead of its yellow, so it doesn't look like the Karoo's own graph. Detailed colours the slope by grade with the Karoo's climb colours, like Mountaineer. Both: the ridden part greyed, ▼ is you.

3b · At the top (Settings)

The rider picks what the header shows: the next cut-off, feed stop, water or toilet (or nothing), as a distance or as a time. Time is when you reach a cut-off (green if before it, red if after) or how long until the next stop. It comes from your own speed on this ride per grade (so riding in a bunch on the flat counts), with Mountaineer's power physics where there's nothing learned yet. Here the showcase clock (10:24 at km 24.6) is late for a real 312 start, so the 11:20 cut shows red.

4 · Map

The Karoo draws every route POI itself (grey pins, its own icons), and an extension can't hide or change them. UpAhead adds only what the Karoo can't show: a small badge on the pin's corner for timing and cut-offs (like the Karoo's own pin badges), and the timed section as black dashes on the route.
